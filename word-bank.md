# Netera Duel — Word Bank Research (Starter Draft)

Single-word slang/meme terms only, grouped by the five eras. This is a **first-pass draft** pulled mainly from Wikipedia's slang glossaries, BuzzFeed's "decade-defining words" retrospective, and a handful of slang-history sites — good enough to prototype the game with, but not yet validated against the 40–60-words-per-band target or checked for cross-era overlap (several terms below genuinely span two bands and need a judgment call on which era owns them).

## Notes for next pass
- **Overlap risk:** several words genuinely straddle two bands (e.g. "lit" and "yeet" bridge Era 3/4, "cheugy" and "sus" bridge Era 4/5, "swag"/"swagger" bridges Era 1/2). Worth deciding a tie-break rule — e.g. assign to the year the term *peaked* rather than first appeared.
- **Sourcing quality:** Wikipedia's "Glossary of 2020s slang" is excellent and citation-heavy — Era 5 above is the most reliable list. Eras 1–3 leaned on SEO listicle sites of mixed reliability and should be cross-checked against better sources (Green's Dictionary of Slang, Merriam-Webster's slang dictionary, Know Your Meme) before finalizing.
- **Multi-word terms excluded per your v1 rule:** e.g. "on fleek," "epic fail," "no cap," "glow up," "fanum tax," "crash out," "locked in," "nepo baby," "hawk tuah" — all real and iconic for their eras, but excluded here since they're phrases, not single words. Worth revisiting if you ever loosen that constraint.
- **Coverage:** this draft sits at roughly 20 words per era (~100 total) — a good chunk short of the 40–60/band target, so there's real room for a deeper pass.
